"Are you going to tell me where you were last night?" Martha asked, folding her arms.

"Oh, certainly," said Tom. "I was at the opera. With the Queen. She sends her warmest regards."

"Don't you dare make a joke of this! I waited up until two in the morning, Tom. Two!"

He looked down at the floor. "I know. I'm sorry. I'm not sure I can explain it, not properly."

"Then try," she said, more quietly now.

"I think," he began, and stopped. He glanced at the window. "I think someone has been following me. For a week, perhaps longer. Or perhaps I've imagined the whole thing. I honestly don't know anymore."
