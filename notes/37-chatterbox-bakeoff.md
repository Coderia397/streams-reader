# Chatterbox expressiveness bakeoff

Plan and results for Chatterbox (original 0.5B, Turbo 350M, Multilingual V3 0.5B) on a private Kaggle T4 kernel, scored with `tools/expressiveness/prosody.py` against the Kokoro baseline (notes/20). Conclusion: original 0.5B at exaggeration 0.5-0.7 per segment gives 80-87 % (female) and 126-130 % (male) of the human prosody index with WER <= 2 % and UTMOS 4.2-4.4; Turbo plain gives 91 / 104 % at RTF 0.45. Per-sentence direction and Turbo tags do not help; exaggeration 0.9 raises F0 and clips. All MIT.

### Findings
- verified: exaggeration 0.9 raises the F0 median (female 227 -> 246 Hz, male 111 -> 129 Hz, +2.6 st) and F0 level sd to 3.40 st (human 1.80); female UTMOS drops 4.28 -> 3.92. EI above 100 % here is overacting, not a better narrator.
- verified: original and MTL outputs saturate (|x| >= 0.99): e0.5 female 5/12 clips, e0.9 male 12/12 clips with 3217 samples; Turbo 0 clips (peak <= 0.84, it loudness-normalises the reference). Measured on the raw 24 kHz FLAC before levelling (scratchpad cbclip.py).
- verified: per-sentence generation hallucinates on 1-3 word sentences: "Two!" -> "Digadoo!" (orig female), "Two. Two." (Turbo, MTL male), "I know." -> "I know. I know my stock. What an APC." (MTL female), "very slowly to turn, very slowly to turn" (orig female); s/syllable rises 0.31 -> 0.39 (orig male). Per-segment generation does not show this.
- verified: Turbo tags lower EI (75 vs 91 % female) and stretch short items ([angry] Two! 3.9x the median duration).
- verified: the female reference (64 kbps LibriVox MP3) has 6-8 kHz energy at -45 dB vs -26 dB for the male reference; every female clone inherits it (orig -47, Turbo -46 dB; Kokoro -31 dB). The female voices will sound dull; a cleaner female reference is needed before judging female quality.
- assumed: the male % is helped by the Walden reader's own variability being cloned; the clone keeps the reference's style.
